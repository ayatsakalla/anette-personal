ABOUT ANETTE

Where ancient wisdom meets the realities of everyday life.

There is a point in life when the usual answers stop working. Not because something is wrong — but because you have outgrown them.

People arrive at this work from different places. Some are already in motion — accomplished, clear-eyed, living a life others consider successful — yet know with quiet certainty there is a deeper level they have not yet reached. Some arrive with a specific question, a decision that needs clarity — a career, a relationship, a moment that asks them to choose. Others come because a pattern keeps repeating: the same wall at different stages, the same result no matter how many times they change the circumstances.

What Anette offers is precision. Drawing from Numerical Psychology — rooted in Sankhya Shastra, one of the oldest frameworks for understanding human nature — alongside Vedic Philosophy and Karmic Management, her work reveals a complete and accurate map of who you are: your nature, your timing, your blind spots, and the depth most people never access.

The work is built entirely around you. Your date of birth. Your unique energetic blueprint. Your life — not a template of it.

Anette does not tell clients what they want to hear; she shows them what they need to see.

Among those she advises are individuals who carry significant responsibility — for organizations, for families, and for outcomes that extend far beyond themselves. The clarity they gain does not stay with them alone. It moves through every decision they make, every room they enter, and everyone within it.

Anette works with a private international clientele across Europe, the Middle East, and the United States, offering confidential sessions in English, Polish, and Russian.
